# Script: The Authentic FOMO Process

**Placement:** Chapter 2, What Is Authentic FOMO? (line ~2886 in fundraising-playbook.html)
**Target length:** ~1:35 to 1:45

---

Authentic FOMO is the single most important concept in this playbook. Understanding it is critical to your success.

So what is it?

Most first-time fundraisers take meetings sporadically. A few here, a few there, spread across months. They never hit enough volume in one window to generate real urgency.

Authentic FOMO solves that. It concentrates your meetings into a short window so urgency builds naturally.

Authentic is the key word. No artificial deadlines. No fake scarcity. Just genuine demand any investor can verify. It describes the demand, and it describes how you show up inside it.

Mechanically, it's straightforward. Bank eighty to one hundred and twenty promised intros before anything goes out, then fire everything at once. That's what packs thirty to fifty plus meetings into two to three weeks.

The contrast with manufactured FOMO is sharp. Manufactured FOMO uses artificial deadlines, fake scarcity, and smoke and mirrors. It falls apart under scrutiny. Authentic FOMO is built on genuine round traction, real investor interest, and compressed timelines. It gets stronger under scrutiny.

Once you understand this, the rest of the playbook starts to make sense. Every step is in service of this compression.

## AI Voiceover Notes

- "FOMO" pronounces cleanly as "foh-moh" in modern voices. If Golpo trips on it, swap to F.O.M.O. with periods for the first instance only
- Numbers spelled out ("eighty to one hundred and twenty," "thirty to fifty plus," "two to three weeks") because AI voices read written numbers more reliably than dashed ranges like "80–120." If your Golpo voice handles digits well, swap them back
- "So what is it?" reads as a soft rhetorical lift before the explanation. The question mark gives the voice an intonation cue
- Short declarative fragments in the "Authentic is the key word" section ("No artificial deadlines. No fake scarcity. Just genuine demand…") are deliberate. Each gets its own beat, builds rhythm
- Closing handoff "Up next, how a round actually gets raised." should be read in one breath, no comma break
- Contractions kept throughout for conversational cadence
- "Smoke and mirrors" is idiomatic and reads naturally in modern voices
