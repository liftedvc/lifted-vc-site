# Script: Map Your Connectors

**Placement:** Chapter 3 primer video, top of sub-1-0
**Target length:** ~90 to 110 seconds

---

You have your target list. Now you need a way in. That's what this chapter is about. Mapping a warm path to every investor on your list.

A connector is someone with a genuine, warm relationship to a partner at one of your target funds. Not a mutual LinkedIn connection. Not someone who knows of the firm. A real relationship the partner would recognize and respond to. Who introduces you matters as much as who you're introduced to.

Connectors fall into four tiers. Tier zero, existing investors already in your round. Most powerful, but most first-time founders won't have them. Tier one, exited founders who returned capital to the fund. Highest trust, rare. Tier two, current portfolio founders. This is your primary pipeline. Most accessible, most scalable, and where the majority of your intros will come from. Tier three, service providers and friends. Bonus only.

Two rules that make this work. First, always target a specific named partner. The general partner is the only person who can say yes. Someone at Benchmark is not an intro to Peter Fenton. Second, one connector per investor. Pick the strongest path. Don't multi-thread until you need to.

Once you know who your connectors are, you need to find them. Start with your cap table, then cross-reference LinkedIn and portfolio databases like Crunchbase. For each investor, run a five-step audit. Identify the partner. Check your existing investors. Map portfolio founders. Pick the strongest connector. And if there's no path, flag it and come back later.

You are not done until 75% of your investor list has a Tier zero through two connector identified, and you have a realistic path to 80 or more promised intros. If you can't get there, go back and expand the list. This step is where most founders under-invest. Don't be one of them.
