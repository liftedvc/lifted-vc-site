# Script: The Four Goals

**Placement:** Chapter 1, sub-arc-2: The Four Goals
**Target length:** ~90 to 100 seconds
**Character count:** 1,520

---

When running the Authentic FOMO method, you are optimizing for four outcomes.

First, you control your timing. You decide when the round opens, and you control the scheduling of each pitch. Double opt-in introductions let you hold every intro until you're ready. Line them up, then fire everything at once. Leads and first-check writers go early. Follow-on investors come later. You set the pace.

Second, you generate real momentum. When you pitch 30 to 50 investors inside a two to three week window, you create engagement you can honestly talk about. Think about it from the investor's side. When a company already has everyone's attention, it naturally carries more weight. Saying you have 40 investor meetings upcoming signals they may need to move quickly.

Third, immediate authenticity. You show up honestly. You have the confidence to be exactly who you are today. Authenticity is the root of trust, and investors feel it before they hear it. When you have 30 to 50 meetings on the calendar, the activity speaks for itself. You can just be real.

Fourth, you end up with the right cap table. When you see investor behavior side by side, the patterns jump out. Who responds quickly. Who asks thoughtful questions. Who stays engaged after the first meeting. You get to pick the investors who treat you best and weigh their value to the company.

Authentic FOMO is designed to get you to these four outcomes. The more you internalize them now, the more naturally they'll show up when you're in front of investors.
