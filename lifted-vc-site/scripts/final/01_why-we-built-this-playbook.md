# Why We Built This Playbook

**Placement:** Landing page hero (line ~2670)
**Length:** ~1:25 to 1:35

---

There's no shortage of fundraising advice out there. Most of it is fragmented. Some of it is wrong. And almost none of it shows you how a real round actually gets raised, end to end.

That's why I built this playbook.

I founded and sold a YC company. I raised from top-tier funds. I spent years on the investor side. And over the last two years, I've coached more than a hundred founders through their pre-seed and seed rounds. They've raised over eighty million dollars. From Y Combinator, Andreessen Horowitz, Pear Ventures, Bessemer, Founder Collective, Hustle Fund, Techstars, and Khosla.

This playbook is the curriculum I wish I'd had on my own raises.

Eleven interactive chapters. Built from real raises, not theory. Every framework here came out of an actual coaching session with a real founder closing a real round. The patterns are the ones that showed up across hundreds of meetings, term sheets, and closes.

It's stage agnostic, pre-seed to Series A. Where the specifics differ by stage, like traction bars, check sizes, or what investors are evaluating, I call it out directly.

If you're raising, this is the curriculum. Hit Get the playbook below, and let's get to work.
