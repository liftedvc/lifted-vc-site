# Collateral Prioritization

**Placement:** Chapter 4 → Prepare Your Materials → Collateral Prioritization
**Length:** ~1:00 to 1:10

---

This chapter walks through the materials you'll build for your raise: the elevator pitch, the blurb, the deck, the one-pager, the data room, and the demo. Each sub-chapter goes deep on one piece. What you actually build depends on your raise, your stage, and your situation.

Get the most out of this chapter by building in parallel with your investor outreach. The materials and the conversations inform each other. Early conversations sharpen the materials. The materials open the door to more conversations.

A few principles for how to think about it.

Collateral gets built in iterations. Draft, send, listen, sharpen across investor conversations. Every conversation is feedback for the next version.

Start with the foundation. The elevator pitch and the blurb tend to be the root of everything else, so they earn the first hours of work.

Over-preparing is one of the easiest traps in fundraising. The strongest materials come from shipping, listening, and revising in real conversations.
