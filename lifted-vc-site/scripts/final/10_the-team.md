# The Team

**Placement:** Chapter 3 → Communicate Your Story → The Team
**Length:** ~1:50 to 2:00

---

At pre-seed and seed, investors are mostly buying belief in the people. Product data is thin, traction is partial. The team becomes the most durable signal about whether this company can be built.

Lead with founder-market fit. The case that your specific background, experience, and stake in this problem make your team the right one to solve it. Open with the connection between your background and the problem, before any job title. If you've lived the customer pain, say so plainly. If you've built or sold into this market before, lead with that.

When you talk about credentials, specific accomplishments beat titles. "Maya built the credit underwriting system at Brex that processes four billion in annual transactions" lands far stronger than "Maya was Director of Engineering at Brex for three years." Lead with the system shipped, the deal closed, the team scaled, the metric moved.

Prove the co-founder relationship will hold. Co-founder dysfunction is the most common cause of cascading damage at early-stage companies. The way to address it is shared history. You worked together before. You've shipped something together. You've already navigated a real disagreement on this company and resolved it cleanly.

Show what your team has produced since starting. Customer conversations completed, prototypes built, design partners signed, hires made. Past founder behavior is the strongest signal of future founder behavior.

The team narrative answers one question: why these people, for this problem, right now.
