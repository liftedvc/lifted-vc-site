## Script

See the round from the investor's side of the table. There are three main forces that shape how a VC thinks about your deal. Once you know them, Authentic FOMO tactics make sense.

Force one. Asymmetric risk.

A VC's worst nightmare is realizing they passed on the next unicorn. Second-worst is a company they backed failing. But missing the unicorn follows them for their whole career, and a failed bet is part of the game.

So while a VC considers whether to invest, they're optimizing to avoid missing the unicorn. You need to frame your company as a unicorn, where there is massive upside.

Force two. Now or never.

Early stage investing is different from public stocks, which reprice every second. Missing the price on a public stock is no biggie. The next opportunity is right around the corner. Whereas early stage investments reprice every twelve to eighteen months, and the next number is usually materially higher.

If a VC passes on your seed at twenty million post, the next chance is your Series A at forty or one hundred million. The VC who passes on you today knows it's probably their only opportunity to invest in your company.

Force three. Coordinated volume.

One fund with conviction might get you funded. Two or more funds with conviction is a competitive process where every fund is calibrating against losing the deal. This is an exponential benefit to you: faster closes, better terms, and getting to choose the best investor for your company.

Every Authentic FOMO tactic exists to engineer that condition. Two or more funds landing conviction at the same time.
