# Best Practices (Intro)

**Placement:** Chapter 3 → Communicate Your Story → Best Practices
**Length:** ~1:00 to 1:05

---

Communicating well is a real skill, and it's worth practicing because investor facetime is limited. A first meeting is often just 30 minutes. In that window, you have to deliver enough for an informed decision about moving you forward in their process.

What you're aiming for is ultimate clarity and authenticity: clarity so the investor can follow exactly what you've built, authenticity so they can trust the source.

The strategy is to lay out enough evidence that the investor reaches your conclusion on their own. Mastering how you convey a specific customer story, a named metric, or a sharp number with the right label is as important as the information itself.

What follows helps you design how you communicate your company across any investor-facing format: pitching, pitch decks, or anything else.
