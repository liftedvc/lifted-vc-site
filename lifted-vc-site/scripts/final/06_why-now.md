# Why Now

**Placement:** Chapter 3 → Communicate Your Story → Why Now
**Length:** ~1:45 to 1:55

---

Why Now is your case for why this moment matters for your product.

The strongest Why Now arguments share a structure. They start from a single foundation, anchor with specifics, and stack additional foundations if they apply.

Start by picking the foundation. Here are the three most common types.

First. Cost dropped. A specific input cost crossed a threshold, and the unit economics now work for the first time. Think AWS and the SaaS wave.

Second. Tech arrived. A new capability became available that your product fundamentally requires. Think smartphones for Uber.

Third. Buyer changed. Demand shifted. A new buyer emerged and now needs this urgently. Think video chat platforms during COVID.

Pick the one that is most true for you.

Then anchor it with specifics. Pick the cost, the technical threshold, or the event that activated your moment. "AI is going to change everything" gets skipped past. "Remote work jumped to sixty percent in March 2020" lands.

And stack as many foundations as you can honestly claim. Stacking has a compounding effect. One can be argued away. Two are hard to dismiss. Three make the moment feel inevitable.
