## Script

An Authentic FOMO raise has three phases. Preparation is the foundation. Activation is the hinge that compresses everything. The close turns momentum into wires.

Phase one. Preparation. Four parallel tracks: build your target list, map your connectors, prepare your materials, and craft your pitch. Each track runs independently, but often simultaneously.

Phase two. Activation. Send your introduction requests within a short window, enabling you to schedule all your pitches over a 2 to 3 week period. The compression is what supports Authentic FOMO.

Phase three. The close. Pipeline management takes over: diligence, term sheet, and the wire. Multiple investors in overlapping stages, tracked rigorously.

Build the right list. Line up great connectors. Create effective collateral. Fire the intros in a coordinated wave. And you will see real momentum take shape.
