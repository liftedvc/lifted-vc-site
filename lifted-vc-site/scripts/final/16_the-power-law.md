## Script

Founders who understand how venture capital works tend to run better fundraising processes. This chapter is a brief overview of the math and mechanics behind investor decisions. A foundation for the rest of the playbook.

Start with the most important number in venture: VC returns follow a power law.

A small number of investments produce the bulk of the returns. The rest lose money or break even. That's the math the venture model is built around, and it shapes almost everything that happens inside a fund.

Most investments lose money. In fact, fifty-one percent of the dollars invested in U.S. venture over the past decade lost money. A successful fund will see the lion's share of returns come from its top four percent of investments. A fund only works if it can land those top outcomes.

That's the math.

Your job is to credibly show that your company could be one of that four percent. That's what investors are searching for. And it's what the rest of this playbook is in service of.
