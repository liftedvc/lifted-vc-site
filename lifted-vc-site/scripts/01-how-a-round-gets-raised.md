Here's how a round actually gets raised, and why running it in the right sequence is what creates real investor urgency.

Every raise follows three phases.

Phase one is preparation. Four parallel tracks: build your target list, map your connectors, prepare your materials, and craft your pitch. Each track runs independently, but often simultaneously.

Phase two is activation. The strategy is to send most of your introduction requests within a short time window. That concentrates your meetings into a small window too. And that compression is what supports Authentic FOMO.

Phase three is the close. Pipeline management takes over. Diligence, term sheet negotiation, and the wire. Multiple investors in overlapping stages, tracked rigorously.

The hinge of the whole process is when you activate.

Send intros one at a time and your meetings land one at a time across months. Lock up eighty to one hundred and twenty promised intros first, then send most of them in a single window, and your meetings compress into a tight schedule.

Authentic FOMO comes from that compression. Investor demand was already real. Concentrating the meetings just makes it visible.

## AI Voiceover Notes

- "Phase one. / Phase two. / Phase three." pattern is on purpose so each phase gets a hard stop and lands as its own beat. Resist reformatting to colons or commas
- Numbers spelled out ("eighty to one hundred and twenty") because AI voices read written numbers more reliably than dashed ranges. If your Golpo voice handles digits well, swap back
- "FOMO" pronounces cleanly as "foh-moh" in modern voices. If Golpo trips on it, swap to F.O.M.O. with periods for the first instance only
- The long sentence "Lock up eighty to one hundred and twenty promised intros first, then send most of them in a single window, and your meetings compress into a tight schedule." has comma pauses built in. If the voice runs flat through it, break it into three sentences at the commas
- "Tracked rigorously" should land as the close phrase of the close-phase paragraph. Brief beat after for emphasis
- Closing handoff "Up next, the four outcomes you get when this runs right." should be read in one breath
- Contractions kept throughout for conversational cadence
