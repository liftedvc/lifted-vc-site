# Script: What a Connector Is

**Placement:** Chapter 3, sub-1-0: What a Connector Is
**Target length:** ~90 to 100 seconds

---

A connector is someone with a genuine, warm relationship to a partner at one of your target funds. Not a mutual LinkedIn connection. Not someone who knows of the firm. A real relationship the partner would recognize and respond to.

This is the most important concept in the entire fundraising process. Who introduces you matters as much as who you're introduced to.

Connectors fall into four tiers. Tier zero, existing investors. People who have already written you a check. They carry institutional weight and are your most powerful lever. Tier one, exited founders. Founders who successfully exited or returned capital to the fund. These are an investor's highest trust sources. Rare, but powerful. Tier two, current portfolio founders. Founders currently funded by the target fund. This is your primary pipeline. Most accessible, most scalable, and where the majority of your intros will come from. Tier three, service providers and friends. Lawyers, accountants, casual contacts. Gets you in the door, but doesn't move the needle. Bonus only.

Most first-time founders won't have Tier zero. That's fine. Focus on Tier two. Portfolio founders are often ready to introduce you, but founders rarely ask. They have a solid relationship with the investor and understand the fund thesis. Systematic outreach to portfolio founders converts better than any other connector strategy.

One more thing. Connectors don't introduce you to someone at the fund. They introduce you to a specific named partner. The general partner is the decision maker. The only person who can say yes. Always name the specific partner when asking for an intro. Someone at Benchmark is not an intro to Peter Fenton.
