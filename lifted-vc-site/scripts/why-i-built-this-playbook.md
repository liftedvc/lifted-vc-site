Mastering fundraising is a critical skill for founders. But fundraising can feel like a black box. Great advice is everywhere. So is bad advice. It's hard to tell which is which, and even harder to know what applies to you.

So I built this playbook. It covers the experience from end to end.

This is the exact method I've used with all my clients in over three hundred coaching sessions. The founders I've coached have raised over eighty million dollars from amazing VCs like YC, Andreessen Horowitz, Pear Ventures, Bessemer, Founder Collective, Hustle Fund, and many, many more.

Every tactic here is grounded in what actually works in a real raise. These patterns showed up across hundreds of investor meetings. Every raise has sharpened the method.

It also pulls from my own founder experience and the best frameworks from top investors online.

The playbook has thirteen chapters, end to end, from kickoff to wired check. It's stage agnostic from pre-seed to Series A. And when you can see the whole experience, you do each step better.

And it's free, permanently. This playbook started as curriculum for my clients, but then I decided it should be for everyone. Everything here is already out there in the venture community, and this playbook just brings it together. Every founder deserves access.

These are the techniques that work for founders, and they're the same techniques investors have been calling for. Now they're finally packaged so you can execute.

So hit Access the playbook below, and let's get to work.
