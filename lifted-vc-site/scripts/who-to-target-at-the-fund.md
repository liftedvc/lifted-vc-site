# Script: Who to Target at the Fund

**Placement:** Chapter 6 (Build Your Target List), 6.03 Who to Target at the Fund (line ~4877 in fundraising-playbook.html). Video title on page: "Who to target at the fund."

There is really only one target: Partners. Ninety-nine percent of the time, only partners write checks. Everyone else at the fund routes, screens, or assists.

Two categories at any fund. Partners sign off on deals, champion deals internally, and write the checks. Your connector's credibility only carries if it's meaningful to the partner specifically.

Junior VCs are associates, analysts, principals, and VPs. They source, screen, and filter. Some can champion internally. Partners are the ones who carry the deal through the partnership meeting and write the check. An intro to a junior starts you on the filter side of the funnel.

So when a connector offers a junior intro, redirect.

Here's the script. "Thanks so much. Out of respect for everyone's time, I try to get introduced directly to the partner who covers my sector and stage at the firm. Is there a specific partner you'd recommend, or someone who could make that intro?"

Acknowledge warmly to keep the connector on your side. Name the reason. Ask for the partner specifically. Put the ball back on the connector to facilitate.

Two exceptions. If fund fit is unclear, a junior meeting works as qualification plus pitch practice. Take it and pursue a parallel partner intro. And once a partner has taken the meeting and hands you to an associate for follow-up diligence, that's standard process. The partner is still your champion.
